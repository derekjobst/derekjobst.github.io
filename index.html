Derek Jobst

Derek leads Product Security at Notion, where his team works to make the secure path the easy one. That work currently includes building Enterprise Key Management (EKM) and securing Notion's agentic AI products.

Previously:

  • Palantir Technologies, where he worked on developer tools, infrastructure, and software supply chain security
  • RaiseMe, a financial technology startup working to improve equal access to higher education
  • Applecart, a data analytics firm focused on voter engagement

He graduated from the University of Pennsylvania School of Engineering and Applied Sciences in 2017 with a B.S.E. in Computer Science. In college, Derek was involved with Wharton Entrepreneurship, the Penn Tech Trek, PennApps, University Television, and the Bell Society.
